About ELTD

ELTD Music Label is a cutting-edge independent label dedicated to fostering creativity and innovation in music. We specialize in discovering unique voices across multiple genres, from original compositions to reimagined covers.

Our mission is to provide artists with the platform, resources, and creative freedom they need to share their authentic sound with the world. We believe in the power of music to connect, inspire, and transform lives.

Founded on the principles of artistic integrity and creative collaboration, ELTD has become a home for both emerging talents and established artists looking to push boundaries and explore new sonic territories.
